## My Approach

I believe good technology starts with understanding the problem.

Rather than building technology for its own sake, I want to understand:

* What problem are we solving?
* Who are we solving it for?
* How does the current process work?
* What information and data are involved?
* What should the system do?
* Where can technology create meaningful improvement?
* When does AI actually add value?

My goal is to develop both the **technical ability to build systems** and the **business perspective to know what should be built**.
